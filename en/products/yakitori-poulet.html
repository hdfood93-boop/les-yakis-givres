Chicken Yakitori Skewers

6 marinated chicken skewers with a sweet soy sauce.

Chicken marinated in a balanced, tasty soy sauce. The Yakis Givrés classic.

  • Halal (Achahada)
  • Pre-cooked and frozen
  • 250 g · 6 pieces
  • Made in France
  • 100% handmade

Cooking instructions

  • In the oven

    10 - 12 min

    Preheat your oven to 180 °C (gas mark 6). Remove the skewers from their plastic wrapping and place them, still frozen, on a tray lined with baking paper or in an ovenproof dish. Coat all sides with sauce and keep the rest. Place in the middle of the oven and reheat for 10 to 12 minutes in a conventional oven.

  • In a frying pan

    4 - 5 min

    Move the yakitoris to the fridge 2 hours in advance. Heat a little fat in a frying pan, then cook the yakitoris for 4 to 5 minutes, turning them regularly.

  • In the microwave

    3 - 4 min

    Remove the skewers from their plastic wrapping and place them, frozen, on a plate. Heat uncovered in a microwave (900 W) for 3 to 4 minutes. Leave to rest for 1 to 2 minutes, then serve with the sweet soy sauce.

Ingredients

Chicken meat 85% (EU), sweet SOY sauce (SOY sauce (water, SOY, WHEAT flour (GLUTEN), salt)), salt, lemongrass, spices (garlic, ginger).
Sweet soy sauce (10 g): soy sauce 36% (water, SOY, WHEAT flour (GLUTEN), salt), sugar 25%, glucose-fructose syrup, water, acidity regulator: lactic acid.

Allergens: Soy, wheat (gluten).
