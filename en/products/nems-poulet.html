Chicken Spring Rolls (Nems)

About 20 halal chicken spring rolls, 800 g pack.

A timeless classic, crispy and full of flavour. A taste of Asia at home! The number of pieces may vary with weight.

  • Halal (Achahada)
  • Frozen
  • 800 g · ≈ 20 pieces
  • Made in France

Cooking instructions

  • Deep-fry

    10 min

    Without thawing, deep-fry the spring rolls for 10 minutes at 160 °C.

Ingredients

Rice paper 20% (rice, tapioca, water, salt), chicken 42% (origin EU), white cabbage, SOY vermicelli, onion, grated carrot, sunflower oil, sugar, flavour enhancer: E621, FISH sauce (ANCHOVY extract, water, salt, sugar), corn starch, pepper, salt, garlic.

Allergens: Soy, fish (anchovy).

May contain traces of crustaceans, molluscs, celery and soy.
