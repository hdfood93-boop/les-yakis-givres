Chicken Meatball Yakitori

6 cooked tsukune chicken skewers with a sweet soy sauce.

Delicious Japanese-style chicken meatballs, tsukune style, with their sweet soy sauce.

  • Halal (Achahada)
  • Cooked and frozen
  • 250 g · 6 pieces
  • Made in France
  • 100% handmade

Cooking instructions

  • In the oven

    11 - 14 min

    Preheat your oven to 180 °C (gas mark 6). Place in the middle of the oven and reheat for 11 to 14 minutes in a conventional oven, or 11 to 12 minutes in a fan oven. Take the skewers out and coat them with sweet soy sauce, turning them to cover well. Leave to rest for a few minutes before serving.

  • In the microwave

    2 - 3 min

    Take the skewers out of the freezer 2 hours in advance, then heat for 2 min 30 at 900 W. Drizzle with sweet soy sauce after taking them out of the microwave.

Ingredients

Chicken 68% (EU), sweet SOY sauce (SOY sauce (water, SOY, WHEAT flour (GLUTEN), salt)), sugar, EGG, onion, rapeseed oil, salt, spices (ginger and ground black pepper), panko breadcrumbs.
Sweet soy sauce (10 g): soy sauce 36% (water, SOY, WHEAT flour (GLUTEN), salt), sugar 25%, glucose-fructose syrup, water, acidity regulator: lactic acid.

Allergens: Soy, wheat (gluten), egg.
